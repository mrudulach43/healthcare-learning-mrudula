
## My Initial Understanding

Both C-CDA and FHIR are important healthcare interoperability 
standards. C-CDA focuses on exchanging complete clinical documents, 
while FHIR provides a more flexible approach to accessing specific 
healthcare information through APIs.

Coming from a technology background, I find FHIR particularly 
interesting because it uses familiar technologies such as REST 
APIs, JSON, and system integrations. I look forward to understanding 
how both standards work together in real-world healthcare environments.
